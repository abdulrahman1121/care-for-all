## How these were checked (2026-11-22)
- Read each photo page's licence field. Some search results looked free but were **Unsplash+ (paid)**, with a different licence (`unsplash.com/plus/license`); 3 of 8 candidates were, and were rejected.
- Downloaded as 1200px-wide JPEGs through Unsplash's image CDN (`w=1200&q=75&fm=jpg`). That is the only change made; the images were not otherwise edited. Both files are real JPEGs (1200x800) with no GPS or location metadata.

## Known limits
- The Unsplash License says **nothing about model releases**: it does not promise the people shown agreed to appear in a commercial product. Both photos show children. Do not use them in a way that implies the people endorse CareForAll, and revisit this before launching commercially.
- Unsplash can remove a photo at the photographer's request; keep this file up to date if one is replaced.
